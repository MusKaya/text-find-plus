# Privacy policy

Text Find+ collects nothing and sends nothing. The only things it stores are your theme choice,
and only when it is Light or Dark (Automatic stores nothing), and whether you left the regex cheat
sheet open, and only when you did (a closed one stores nothing). Both are kept on your device, in
the popup's own local storage, and need no permission. It makes no network requests, uses no analytics and
loads no remote code. It runs only on the tab where you open it, and only after you click its icon
or press its shortcut. Your search text is used only to find matches on that page. While highlights
are shown, the page's own scripts can see which text is highlighted, and so could work out what you
searched for on that page. So that reopening the popup can restore your last search, the search is
kept in that open page's memory. It is never written to disk and is gone as soon as the page
reloads, navigates or closes.
